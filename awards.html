Awards & Recognition

Recognized for our contribution to responsible recycling and a cleaner future.

A collection of milestones, national challenges, and innovation honors celebrating our dedication to smart, eco-friendly waste management across Bangladesh.

ICT Division · iDEA Project • August 2025

Official Recycling Partner — University Activation Programme 2025

Served as the Official Recycling Partner at the University Activation Programme 2025 (Dhaka Division), hosted by the iDEA Project, ICT Division, at UITS University.

Innovation & Green Tech • November 2025

Exhibitor — Innoverse Bangladesh 2025

Showcased the smart waste management solution to students, industry experts, and innovation leaders at the two-day Innoverse Bangladesh 2025 exhibition.

National IT Showcase • April 2026

Project Showcase — Broadband Expo 2026

Selected for the Project Showcase Category at Broadband Expo 2026, inaugurated by the Hon'ble State Minister for Posts, Telecommunications and IT.

DEIED Project · BHTPA • June 2026

National Innovation Challenge — ICT Division

Recognized at the National Innovation Challenge, powered by the DEIED Project, BHTPA, held at Dhaka University.

International Rural Development • July 2026

International Award — CIRDAP Innovation Challenge 2026

Won the International Award at the CIRDAP International Rural Development Innovation Challenge 2026, presented by Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir.

Global Social Impact • March 2026

2nd Runner-up — Hult Prize UITS 2026

Placed 2nd Runner-up at Hult Prize UITS 2026, hosted by the University of Information Technology & Sciences (UITS).
